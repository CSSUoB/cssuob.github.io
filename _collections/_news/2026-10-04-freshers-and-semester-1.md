Hello and welcome to everyone! CSS sincerely hopes you've had a great Fresher's at the university, 
getting involved with lots of societies, events, and opportunities! We on committee have worked extremely 
hard to make Fresher's a fantastic, unforgettable week for you, and from the pure engagement you'we've seen, 
we think it's paid off! Of course, Fresher's is just the start of the plethora of things we have lined up 
for this year, so read on and keep an eye out on our socials!

## A Look Back at Fresher's Week

Fresher's Week (and week 1) were jam-packed and we're so grateful it went so smoothly! 
And of course, as is tradition, it started off with the beloved Board Games Night! As usual, we saw the 
CS building fill up with people of all years come together to enjoy playing board games, meet new people, 
and enjoy some free pizza (a common theme at our events, you'll soon come to notice). Whether you came out on 
top as a chess champion, bankrupted all your friends in Monopoly, or successfully got enough laughs to be 
the funniest in Cards Against Humanity, we loved seeing so many of you having fun and getting involved on the 
first day! Even if I did lose every game I played :/ it was a lot of fun! And a big thank you to Tabletop Gaming 
who ran some Blood on the Clocktower games, that proved very popular! If you enjoyed the event, keep an eye out 
as in semester 2, we'll be running it again! And quickly, thinking of pizza, our own Secretary Henry has set up 
the [CSS Pizza Statistics](https://cssbham.com/pizza) page so you can keep track of just how much pizza we end 
up buying this year. Thanks Henry!

Next up, we had lunch in the city centre at the wonderful Alfred Works food hall. After many years of declining 
popularity, this year's new location brought in nearly double the attendees from last year! With my delicious 
tacos still on my mind all this time later, I know I'm not the only one who had some thoroughly good food! 
Whether you had Asian, Italian, American, or another great choice of food, we're glad the event was such a success!

A new event for this year, the Fresher's Quiz proved extremely popular! With rounds themed around Computer Science, 
the University, operating systems, and even famous Joes (I was honoured to be included), there really was something 
for everyone to have a go at. And the scores were incredibly high! We were extremely impressed and proud of the 
knowledge we saw! Although, to whoever thought that I, the Scrum Master, was Donald Duck... I'm not upset, 
just disappointed. I joke, of course; I was actually surprised how many of you remembered me! It's been a pleasure 
so far to have so many people recognise me and wave, say hi, or come for a chat; if you do see me around the CS 
buidling, where I'm always working on my Ph.D., feel free to say hi and have a chat! The more excuses to not do work, 
the better! Anyway, a final congratulations again to Team 0, who won the quiz, getting them CSS branded rubber ducks!

Finally, our last big event was Pub Night on Friday of Week 1, which saw us pile into The Soak Bar & Grill in Selly and 
enjoy some delicious curries, and have a couple drinks to celebrate finishing the first week of lectures. So many people, 
new and existing students, came along and made it a fantastic night - a great way to end out an incredible Fresher's! If 
you enjoyed the pub night, we will be making them a regular event, with them happening every other week (the next 
happening Friday week 3), so come along and chat, relax, and enjoy a couple drinks, whether alcoholic or not!

Throughout the week, we also had other smaller, but equally as important, events happening! Throughout the week, we had 
our stall up in the CS atrium, at the Guild Societies Fair, the School of CS Fair, and the EPS Fair. We had such good 
interest from you all, coming to chat, ask questions, start your TeX sticker collection, and join our socials! If you 
haven't already, check out our website's [links page](https://cssbham.com/links) to join our Discord, WhatsApp, and 
Instagram pages to get all the latest updates about upcoming events! We also ran the annual Scavenger Hunt around campus 
for you to learn some fun facts about the place you'll study at for the next 3 (or more!) years, as well as earn a 
special edition Old Joe TeX sticker to become the ultimate collector of our stickers. This year, we again saw more 
interest than we've had for years, so thank you to everyone who participated! 

Amongst everything that's happened, you may have noticed that we have a modded Java Minecraft server running! All 
the details you need (server address, mod list, rules, etc.) are available on the Discord Minecraft channel. Whether 
you play Minecraft all the time, or you've not played for years, this is a great chance to get stuck in, master magic, 
build the most efficient Create machinery, or like me, just build pretty things! We hope to see some of you in the server!

If you've enjoyed your time in CSS so far, and want to support us - along with getting many benefits - why not get 
yourself a £5 membership at [cssbham.com/join](https://cssbham.com/join). The membership lasts for the whole year, 
and gets you discounts on ticketed events, saving you money! It also gives you a pretty colour on our Discord 
server, a free t-shirt (soon), and the ability to vote at our upcoming EGM. Still unsure? Well, your membership 
goes straight to supporting the society, so we can pay for all the pizza, get you cool merch, and run incredible 
events for you and your friends to enjoy whilst at uni! 

## What's Coming Up in Semester 1?

As usual, we have tonnes of events planned for you this semester on the run-up to Christmas, so let's tease some!

Firstly, we have our Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM) in a couple weeks. The EGM is a meeting for people to run 
for roles on committee, vote on the candidates, and propose constitutional amendments. This event requires attendees 
to be members of the society, but it's a great chance for you to have a chance to say how CSS should be run, as well 
as see some early access sneak peaks at events and, wait, tickets for events?! I wonder what event that will be! 
The EGM will be in the Winifred Hackett Building on Wednesday 21st October from 1pm. If you're interested in 
running or attending, keep an eye out on our socials for more information soon!

Then, on Thursday 29th October at 7pm, we're running our Halloween Quiz; get dressed up in your halloween costume, 
form a team, and enjoy more free pizza! Much like the Fresher's Quiz, we'll be running the quiz in the atrium in teams 
of up to 6, and we'll have free pizza and alcohol on offer like Board Games Night. Perhaps most importantly, there 
will be prizes! The winning team, and the best dressed, will all get prizes; however, we will have Halloween TeX 
stickers available for all who participate in the quiz! Oh, and did I mention? We have guest rounds! A beloved 
member of staff, and a beloved ex-secretary; what more could you want?

Finally, our last big event of semester 1 is my personal favourite: the CSS Christmas Meal! Join us at the Green 
Man in Harborne for delicious food, good vibes, and a celebration of semester 1. This event is ticketed, so keep 
an eye out for when tickets release, to get yourself in on the fun! Of course, we highly recommend getting dressed 
up in your best and ugliest Christmas jumpers (I'll be there in mine, and my Santa hat)! And it'll be your chance 
to get your hands on the Christmas TeX sticker for your collection. The date and time will be revealed soon, so 
keep your eyes peeled! 

Besides our big events, we have plenty more on offer! Maybe you're an arcade bar fan, and are looking for a 
reason to go to NQ64? Perhaps you're looking for a new society, and a collaboration would be the perfect chance 
for you to find one? Or maybe, you're looking to learn a new skill? Well, we've got it all! NQ64 evening, 
workshops, collabs, sponsor events, (did someone say mini hackathon?!), and a whole lot more! We'll announce 
these events as we go through the year, but if you have more event suggestions, don't hesitate to reach out and 
tell us! 

Oh, and t-shirts! As you may have heard us say - and you've likely seen people wearing them - if you get the 
membership for the society, you will get a free t-shirt. Now, we haven't forgotten, I promise! Issues at the start 
of the year pertaining to funds (I don't know how Idris has managed the finances so well!) meant they were delayed, 
however, they have been ordered and are on their way! The moment they arrive, we will be running a big collection 
event in the CS building so you can come and collect the shirt, or buy a membership there and then to get one! 
It even has our sponsors on the back.

We are looking forward to seeing many of you continue to get involved in the society as we go through the year, 
and we can't wait to run these events for you!
